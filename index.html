Assisted Application Support
Government E-Service & Certificates

Applying for Tamil Nadu government welfare certificates and public portals can often be complex. We assist rural and local citizens in filing digital applications accurately on the official e-Sevai portal.

Required Documents for Certificate Applications:

  • Community / Income / Nativity: Ration Card, Aadhaar Card, School TC, and Photo.
  • Patta / Chitta Online Extraction: Survey number, sub-division number, and village details.
  • Voter ID & Smart Card Updates: Existing card proof, address proof, and active mobile number.
  • Online Government Certificate Applications (Income, Community, Nativity)
  • Patta, Chitta, EC (Encumbrance Certificate) Downloads
  • Smart Card (Family Ration Card) Correction & Member Additions
  • Voter ID Registration, Correction, and PVC Card Printing

Fast Processing
New PAN Card & Corrections

Whether you need a new Permanent Account Number (PAN) for banking or need to update your name, photo, or father's name, we provide error-free filing assistance through NSDL/UTIITSL portals.

What You Should Know:

Instant e-PAN can be generated within 1 to 2 hours if your mobile number is linked with Aadhaar. Physical PVC PAN cards will be delivered by Indian Post to your registered doorstep address within 10 to 15 working days.

  • New PAN Card Application for Individuals and Minors
  • Correction of Name, Date of Birth, and Father's Name on PAN
  • Aadhaar-PAN Linking Assistance & e-KYC Verification
